## (b)

The **Cranfield Paradigm** is a foundational experimental methodology in information retrieval that provides a standardized framework for evaluating retrieval systems. It involves:

1. A standard corpus used for all queries
2. A set of standard queries
3. A set of relevance judgements
4. Evaluation measures such as precision and recall.

Its advantages include repeatability, objectivity, and efficiency. 

## (c)

### Information Need: 4 Stages (VCFC)

1. **Visceral need (本能需求)**: the actual, but unexpressed need for information : “a vague sort of dissatisfaction ...  probably inexpressible in linguistic terms”.  
2. **Conscious need (有意识的需求)**: the conscious within-brain description of the need: “an ambiguous and rambling  statement”. i.e. “I know what I’m looking for but I don’t  know how to say it”.  
3. **Formalised need (正式需求):** the formal statement of the question   
4. **Compromised need (折衷需求): t**he question as presented to the information system

#### Hidden Text

**方法**：

- 文字颜色与背景相同（`<font color="#ffffff">`）。
- CSS隐藏（`.class { color: white; }`）。

**目的**：

- 关键词堆砌（Keyword Stuffing）。
- 人为提高词频（TF）。

**检测难点**：需解析CSS或遍历DOM树，增加爬虫开销。

#### **Cloaking**

**原理**：向爬虫提供具有高度针对性的文本，以便在特定搜索中获得高排名，普通用户将获得正常内容

**技术实现**：

- - 根据User-Agent识别爬虫（如Googlebot）。
  - 302重定向至不同页面。
- **示例**：
  - 爬虫收到关键词密集的文本。
  - 用户看到正常电商页面。

#### Sneaky JavaScript

**方法**：

- Web crawlers（和旧浏览器）通常无法执行 JavaScript 代码

- 利用`<noscript>`标签向爬虫展示关键词，用户通过JS跳转到其他页面。
- 爬虫不执行**JavaScript**，仅索引`<noscript>`内容。

#### Exploiting PageRank

**方法**：

- **链接农场（Link Farms）**

  - 一些网站**专门用于链接**其他网站来提高他们的**PageRank**
  - 通常，链接会被隐藏成为**页面相同的颜色**

- **评论垃圾（Comment Spam）**

  在论坛、博客中植入广告链，允许第三方评论的页面被滥用以试图提高 PageRank

#### **Doorway Pages**

- **方法**：
  - 针对不同搜索词创建独立优化页面（如“便宜机票.html”“折扣机票.html”）
  - 所有页面最终跳转到**同一目标页**
- **目的**：覆盖更多搜索词，避免单页关键词稀释

### (ii)
**Stopword revomal:**
- A **stopword** is a commonly occurring term that appears in so many documents that it **does not add to the meaning** of the document, and appear in most English texts. (e.g. *the, and, of, etc.*)

**Zipf’s Law:**
- If we rank terms from the most commonly used to the least commonly-used in  a large text collection, the second term is approximately ½ as common as the  first, the third is approximately ⅓ as common as the first, etc.
- We use this to identify what terms count as stopwords in various languages.

### (iii)
**Stemming**
- **Stemming** is the process that maps these terms to a **common root**.

-  e.g. “computing”, “computer” → “comput”

**Problems:**

- **overstemming**: 去词根后并不关联

- **Example**: consider the words “general” and “generate”

**Lemmatisation**

-  Lemmatisation is a Natural Language Processing (NLP) technique for  converting words into **lemmas**. 

- This is **slower** than stemming, but is usually more **effective**, because it can deal  with words that stemming can’t handle.

### (ii)

#### **1. 预处理查询**
- **去除停用词** (`a, an, is, some, the`):  
  `"what is her news"` → `["what", "her", "news"]`  
- **保留有效词项**: `"her"`, `"news"`（`"what"` 不在词汇表中，忽略）

#### **2. 查询向量（TF-IDF）**
- **词频（TF）**:  
  - `"her"`: 1/2 = 0.5  
  - `"news"`: 1/2 = 0.5  
- **IDF（沿用之前的log₂结果）**:  
  - `"her"`: 1.585  
  - `"news"`: 0.585  
- **TF-IDF 查询向量**:  
  - `"her"`: 0.5 × 1.585 ≈ 0.793  
  - `"news"`: 0.5 × 0.585 ≈ 0.293  
  - 其他词项: 0  
- **向量表示**:  
  \[
  \mathbf{q} = [\text{her}:0.793, \text{news}:0.293]
  \]

#### **3. 文档向量（TF-IDF log₂）**
沿用之前计算的文档向量（仅保留 `"her"` 和 `"news"` 维度）:

| 词项   | Doc1               | Doc2               | Doc3               |
|--------|--------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| her    | 0.396              | 0                  | 0                  |
| news   | 0.073              | 0.195              | 0                  |

- **向量表示**:  
  - $\mathbf{d}_1 = [\text{her}:0.396, \text{news}:0.073]$
  - $\mathbf{d}_2 = [\text{her}:0, \text{news}:0.195]$
  - $\mathbf{d}_3 = [\text{her}:0, \text{news}:0]$


#### **4. 余弦相似度公式**
$$
\text{cos}(\mathbf{q}, \mathbf{d}) = \frac{\mathbf{q} \cdot \mathbf{d}}{\|\mathbf{q}\| \cdot \|\mathbf{d}\|}
$$
其中：
- $\mathbf{q} \cdot \mathbf{d}$ 为向量点积  
- $\|\mathbf{q}\|$ 和 $\|\mathbf{d}\|$ 为向量的欧几里得范数  

#### **1. 查询向量 $\mathbf{q}$ 的范数**
$$
\|\mathbf{q}\| = \sqrt{0.793^2 + 0.293^2} \approx \sqrt{0.629 + 0.086} \approx 0.846
$$

#### **2. 各文档相似度计算**
**Doc1**:
- 点积：
  $$
  0.793 \times 0.396 + 0.293 \times 0.073 \approx 0.314 + 0.021 = 0.335
  $$
- 文档范数：
  $$
  \sqrt{0.396^2 + 0.073^2} \approx \sqrt{0.157 + 0.005} \approx 0.402
  $$
- 相似度：
  $$
  \frac{0.335}{0.846 \times 0.402} \approx \frac{0.335}{0.340} \approx 0.985
  $$

**Doc2**:
- 点积：
  $$
  0 \times 0.793 + 0.195 \times 0.293 \approx 0 + 0.057 = 0.057
  $$
- 文档范数：
  $$
  \sqrt{0^2 + 0.195^2} = 0.195
  $$
- 相似度：
  $$
  \frac{0.057}{0.846 \times 0.195} \approx \frac{0.057}{0.165} \approx 0.345
  $$

**Doc3**:
- 点积：
  $$
  0 \times 0.793 + 0 \times 0.293 = 0
  $$
- 相似度：
  $$
  0
  $$
