TF saturation, IDF, Document Length normalisation

**TF saturation:** 

There is an upper limit to the contribution of the frequency of occurrence (TF) of terms in the document to relevance. Avoid high-frequency words overly dominating the ranking.

> 词项在文档中出现的频率（TF）对相关性的贡献存在上限，避免高频词过度主导排序
